## English

Chapter 1 of the series **[Discernimiento humano y creatividad soberana mediada por IA](https://github.com/SandraAcevedoA/serie-discernimiento-creatividad)** (*Human discernment and sovereign creativity, mediated by AI*): a three-hour applied workshop taught in Spanish at [The Multiverse School](https://themultiverse.school/classes/289).

**Central question**

> What decisions in AI-mediated work can I recognize, explain, and defend as my own?

This repository mirrors the workshop material that is already public at The Multiverse School. The series repository explains how this chapter connects with the other two.

### Using the web walkthrough offline

`interactive/recorrido-web/index.html` is a single self-contained file. Download it and open it in a browser. Answers written in the lab are stored only in that browser; the lab lets you copy your full record at the end.

### Provenance and credits

- The workshop is a situated adaptation of Liz Howard's *Critical Thinking and Creativity with AI*. The series [`LINEAGE.md`](https://github.com/SandraAcevedoA/serie-discernimiento-creatividad/blob/main/LINEAGE.md) maps which parts of the school's curriculum it touches.
- *Make It Yours* is by Liz Howard. The vocabulary builds on *Critical Thinking Qualities for AI Text* and on Liz Howard's facilitation vocabulary; its own provenance section lists the sources. The annex by modality is Sandra Acevedo's extension for this workshop.
- Panels 3 and 4 of the walkthrough refer to Stephanie Dinkins, *Conversations with Bina48* (2014–), and Holly Herndon, *PROTO* (2019).

### Usar el recorrido web sin conexión

`interactive/recorrido-web/index.html` es un solo archivo autocontenido. Descárgalo y ábrelo en un navegador. Lo que escribas en el laboratorio se guarda solo en ese navegador; al final, el laboratorio permite copiar el registro completo.

### Procedencia y créditos

- El workshop es una adaptación situada de *Critical Thinking and Creativity with AI*, de Liz Howard. El [`LINEAGE.es.md`](https://github.com/SandraAcevedoA/serie-discernimiento-creatividad/blob/main/LINEAGE.es.md) de la serie muestra qué partes del currículo de la escuela toca.
- *Make It Yours* es de Liz Howard. El vocabulario parte de *Critical Thinking Qualities for AI Text* y del vocabulario de facilitación de Liz Howard; su propia sección de procedencia lista las fuentes. El anexo por modalidad es una ampliación de Sandra Acevedo para este workshop.
- Los paneles 3 y 4 del recorrido hacen referencia a Stephanie Dinkins, *Conversations with Bina48* (2014–), y a Holly Herndon, *PROTO* (2019).
